Our Accommodations

Hotel

Discover

Stay at hotels that combine modern comfort with tropical charm, ideally located between the ocean and the city. Perfect for your first visit to the archipelago, they offer quality service, a serene atmosphere, and direct access to must-see attractions.
The perfect balance between local hospitality and high-end amenities.

Bungalows

Discover

Escape to bungalows nestled in the heart of nature, surrounded by tropical gardens, secluded beaches, and equatorial forests. These intimate retreats—often with your feet in the sand or perched high on the hills—promise tranquility, a break from it all, and total immersion in the untamed essence of São Tomé and Príncipe.

Private Villas

Discover

Surrounded by lush nature and overlooking the ocean, they allow you to experience the archipelago in complete freedom, as if you were in a tropical home all to yourself. Discreet luxury set against a pristine backdrop.

Praia Banana (Banana Beach)

Banana Beach, on Príncipe, is a small, quiet beach located on the island of Príncipe, known for its crystal-clear waters, light-colored sand, and lush coconut palms. It’s an ideal spot to relax, swim, and enjoy the island’s unspoiled nature. The beach retains a rustic, unspoiled feel, perfect for those seeking peace and quiet.
Lieu
Oquê Pipi Waterfall (Oquê Pipi Waterfall)

The Oquê Pipi Waterfall is a natural waterfall located in the southern part of Príncipe Island. It is a well-preserved site, surrounded by tropical rainforest, and is a popular destination for nature lovers and hikers.
Lieu
View from Nova Estrela

View from Nova Estrela, where you can admire the famous “Boné de Jóquei.”
Lieu
Roça Sundy

Roça Sundy is a former cocoa plantation located on the island of Príncipe, in São Tomé and Príncipe. Founded during the Portuguese colonial era, the plantation has preserved typical historic buildings, such as the manor house and the cocoa warehouses, offering a glimpse into the architecture and life on the plantations. Today, it is also a tourist and cultural site that allows visitors to learn about the history of cocoa production, the colonial way of life, and the island’s economic importance.
Lieu
Santa Catarina Tunnel

The Santa Catarina Tunnel in São Tomé is a former railway tunnel built during the colonial era to facilitate the transport of agricultural products. Carved out of volcanic rock, it is now a historic and tourist site, renowned for its simple architecture and heritage value.
Lieu
The Santa Catarina Tunnel

The Santa Catarina Tunnel in São Tomé is a former railway tunnel built during the colonial era to facilitate the transport of agricultural products. Carved out of volcanic rock, it is now a historic and tourist site, renowned for its simple architecture and heritage value.
Lieu
Fernão Dias

Not far from Micoló (about 7 minutes away) is Fernão Dias Beach, a site of great historical significance for the islands. It was there that the prisoners from the 1953 Batepá massacre were taken. Today, the site is marked by a memorial bearing the names of all the victims of the massacre.
Lieu
Lagoa Azul (Blue Lagoon)

The Blue Lagoon, in São Tomé, is a natural lagoon with turquoise-blue waters located on the island's northwest coast. It is renowned for its calm, crystal-clear waters, which are ideal for swimming and snorkeling.
Lieu
Roça Agostinho Neto

Roça Agostinho Neto, in São Tomé, is a former colonial cocoa plantation located in the northern part of the island. It is notable for its historic buildings and cultural significance, serving as a symbol of the country’s agricultural past and colonial history.
Lieu
Pico Cão Grande

Pico Cão Grande is an iconic volcanic rock formation in São Tomé, located in the southern part of the island. Rising to a height of approximately 663 meters, it is distinguished by its sharp and imposing shape, making it one of the country’s main natural landmarks.
Lieu
Mangrove Malanza

In the village of Malanza, don’t miss the traditional canoe ride up the Malanza River, home to the country’s largest mangrove area. Protected by Obô Natural Park, this ecosystem is distinguished by its characteristic vegetation, consisting of semi-aquatic trees with aerial roots. Endemic and migratory birds are common here, as are monkeys. It is also home to the famous “cucumbas,” a strange fish capable of living out of water by leaping from root to root. The hike lasts two hours and begins at the bridge over the Malanza River in Porto Alegre.
For more information and to make a reservation, contact Mr. Vadu at 9917602.
Lieu
Praia Jalé (Jalé Beach)

Jalé Beach, located at the southernmost tip of the island of São Tomé, near Porto Alegre, is a paradisiacal natural haven known for its vast stretch of golden sand, its crystal-clear waters, and its vital role in the conservation of sea turtles, which come here to lay their eggs between September and April.
Lieu
São Tomé, Independence Square

Independence Square is the site where national independence was proclaimed on July 12, 1975. Located directly across from Ana Chaves Bay, it is surrounded by banks, stores, and public services. Every year on July 12, Independence Day is celebrated there, and His Excellency the President of the Republic lights the flame of the homeland.
Lieu
Boca do Inferno

"Boca do Inferno," in São Tomé, is a natural coastal formation where waves crash forcefully into cavities in the volcanic rock, creating a spectacular display of water jets. It is a tourist attraction renowned for its natural beauty and the power of the ocean.
Lieu
Roça Água Izé

The Roça Água Izé plantation was the first to begin growing cocoa in São Tomé; with the largest plantations and the most advanced technologies, it had its own dock for exporting cocoa and a long railway line.
Lieu
The Mionga Waterfall

The Mionga Waterfall in São Tomé is a stunning natural waterfall surrounded by dense forest and tropical vegetation.
It’s a peaceful place, ideal for those who love nature, hiking, and getting up close to the outdoors. Reaching the falls usually involves a short walk, which makes the visit even more special for ecotourism and adventure enthusiasts.
Lieu
"Piscina" Beach

The name “Piscina” comes from the natural pools formed by volcanic rocks along the coast, which hold seawater—perfect for a more peaceful and safer swim.
Lieu
Rolas Islet

Rolas Island is a small island located south of São Tomé, straddling the equator. Renowned for its tranquil beaches and unspoiled natural beauty, it is one of the most iconic tourist destinations in São Tomé and Príncipe.
Lieu
Coffee Museum

The Monte Café Coffee Museum in São Tomé is a place dedicated to the history and production of coffee, one of the island's most important crops.
Lieu
Almada Negreiros House-Museum

The Almada Negreiros House-Museum is located very close to the Monte Café plantation (within the Saudade plantation); it chronicles the life and work of José Almada Negreiros, a unique figure in the 20th-century Portuguese art scene, who was born on the island of São Tomé in 1893. Guided tours offer visitors the chance to learn about Almada Negreiros’s life. The museum also serves as a space for exhibiting and promoting the works of artisans and visual artists from São Tomé.
